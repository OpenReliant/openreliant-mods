Jukebox, a mod for OpenReliant
==============================

By OpenReliant, under the Mozilla Public License 2.0.

Its thumbnail, mod.png, and the pictures for its page are frames of the game's CD player as
OpenReliant draws it with the mod, so they show the game's art, by Digital Anvil. The thumbnail is
titled in Newtown, a public domain font by Roger White. sources/jukebox says how they were made.

The mod is free, and needs your own copy of StarLancer.
