Master Recordings, a mod for OpenReliant
========================================

The music of StarLancer, composed by Ian Livingstone, from his original 44.1 kHz master
recordings. Dave Blinston was the audio producer at Warthog.

The recordings remain Ian Livingstone's work, and they aren't under an open licence. On
13 October 2026 he gave his permission to make them available as a mod for OpenReliant that
installs with a single click, as this collection offers it. OpenReliant's maintainer keeps his
written reply.

OpenReliant cut each piece from its master at the start and length of the game's own piece, so
that it loops back where the game's does, and raised its level towards the game's as far as the
master's peaks allowed. The recordings are otherwise unchanged. Takeoff and Searching Mission 10
stay the game's own: no master holds Takeoff, and Searching Mission 10's master is a different
version of the piece.

The CD player's tracks after its 24 play the masters in full, the files named Masters_: the
pieces of the game that it doesn't list, The Search Part 10 (the master of Searching Mission 10),
the music of the movies, and the characters' themes. Each is raised towards the level of the
game's music as far as its peaks allow, and is otherwise unchanged. The album's Reliant Funeral
and Ending have the movies' sounds and voices mixed in, so these two come from the masters of the
music alone.
