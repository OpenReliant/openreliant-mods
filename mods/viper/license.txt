Viper Mk II, a mod for OpenReliant
==================================

This work is based on "Battlestar Galactica Viper Mark 2"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/battlestar-galactica-viper-mark-2-1bf9872a1e8d4b39a259e14a7015a0a2)
by LocoPixel (https://sketchfab.com/locopixel), licensed under CC-BY-NC-4.0
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/).

The model, its textures and every picture made from it (viper.shp, viper_*.png, viperscem_*.png,
viperwire_000.png and vipericon_000.png) are shared under the same licence, CC-BY-NC-4.0: you may
share and adapt them for non-commercial use, crediting LocoPixel as above.

Changes from the original model, by OpenReliant:

- Converted to StarLancer's .SHP format with sltool shp from-gltf, scaled and turned to the game's
  axes, with engine glows, gun muzzles, missile hardpoints, an eject point and jump points added.
- The hull's texture enlarged four times, its stripes made a deeper red, and wear added: grime,
  exhaust streaks, chipped paint, scratches and panel seams with rivets, with a roughness and
  metalness map and a normal map to match.
- The other materials made less shiny.
- The flight display's pictures of the ship (its schematic, its hit markers, the gunnery display's
  wire frame and the wing's icon) drawn from the model.
