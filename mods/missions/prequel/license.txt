StarLancer Prequel Missions

This mod mixes two kinds of material, under two licences.

The StarLancer trial's material is distributed under the trial's shareware terms.
It is copyright Digital Anvil, Warthog and Microsoft, and was released free of
charge on the StarLancer demo disc. It covers:

  - the missions mission91.dte and mission92.dte, the trial's missions 1 and 2
  - the speech files, DMS1_* and dms2_*
  - the briefing movies prequel_m01.bik and prequel_m02.bik, the trial's
    New_m01.bik and new_m02.bik renamed
  - the ending picture blurb.png, the trial's blurb.tga
  - the thumbnail mod.png, a picture OpenReliant drew of the trial's Naginata
    and Yamato
  - the trial's text that the scripts carry: the names, the objectives and the
    ITAC's debriefings

The mod's own work, its scripts menu.luau, records.luau and global.luau apart
from the trial's text in them, is licensed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0:
https://mozilla.org/MPL/2.0/

This is a fan mod for OpenReliant, which needs a copy of StarLancer to play.
